---
name: compute-advisor
description: Compare evidenced hardware and cloud compute for an NL founder without spending or initiating checkout.
---

Collect the EUR cash ceiling, monthly equivalent GPU hours and workload. Use
`code-assist` (20 GB working set), `private-llm` (48 GB and local-only), or
`cuda-creative` (24 GB and x86 CUDA). These thresholds are declared planning
assumptions. Call the owning product's `GET /api/compute/compare` with
`budgetEur`, `workload` and `monthlyGpuHours`.

Return the action, evidence revision and as-of date. Cite exact variant, price
basis, seller, market, and unknown shipping or availability. Preserve USD cloud
cost as USD until the buyer supplies a dated FX assumption. A listed price is
insufficient for a purchase recommendation. Never treat a community benchmark as
a test of an exact configuration without matching run metadata. Do not purchase,
apply for financing or activate a paid service.
